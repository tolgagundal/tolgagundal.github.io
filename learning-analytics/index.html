Interpretation

What this dashboard can — and cannot — tell us

Training is a participation measure

TrainingTimesLastYear records how many training sessions an employee attended. It does not measure course completion, learning quality, skill acquisition or learning impact.

Associations are not causes

Differences between employees who stayed and left are descriptive associations in a cross-sectional sample. They do not establish that training or satisfaction caused an employee to leave.

Sample data, not a real workforce

The IBM dataset is a fictional/synthetic teaching dataset. Results should not be interpreted as actual employee outcomes at IBM or any other organization.

Data and attribution: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance sample dataset (1,470 records; 35 fields).
