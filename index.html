About

A bit about me

I started out in digital banking in Istanbul, moved into Learning & Development and Customer Success in EdTech, and picked up operations coordination along the way — food-tech included. Somewhere in there I got curious about the data behind those workflows, which led me to Berlin for a Master's in Data Analytics, which I completed earlier this year. These days I'm working on data quality at Delivery Hero.

What ties it together is a habit of caring about the details most people skip past — making sure a ticket actually gets resolved, a training actually lands, or a number in a report is actually right. I'm Turkish, speak English fluently, and I'm working on my German (currently B1).
